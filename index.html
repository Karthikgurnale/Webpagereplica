Questions

Before you import

Can I import just one piece?

Yes, for many products. Furniture like height-adjustable desks can be imported with a minimum order of one piece. Private-label products usually start around 500 units.

How long does delivery take?

Most sea shipments reach your door in about 30 to 40 days. Air cargo is faster for urgent orders.

Do you handle customs and GST?

Yes. We prepare the documents and manage duties, GST and clearance, then deliver to your address.

Do I need an Import Export Code (IEC)?

For commercial imports, yes. If you don't have one, we'll guide you through getting it.

Can I visit the factories myself?

Absolutely. Join a guided China business trip, or we arrange visits to the factories you shortlist.
